Monelyra Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026
Last updated: August 22, 2026
Contact: dasguardcore.notify@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Monelyra mobile application handles user data. Monelyra is an Android app designed to help users manage personal finance, reminders, life tracking, backups, and reports. The app is designed with a local-first approach, keeping user data on the device whenever possible.

1. Developer and contact

For privacy, data use, or app-related questions, you can contact the developer/publisher at: dasguardcore.notify@gmail.com.

2. What data may be stored in the app?

Monelyra may store the following types of data entered by the user or created inside the app:

  • Reminder titles, dates, notes, and categories
  • Bill, rent, subscription, vehicle, credit card, and loan tracking information
  • Income, expense, account, debt/receivable, and financial summary information
  • Report data selected by the user for PDF/CSV export
  • App preferences such as theme, language, currency, and security/PIN settings
  • Notification and reminder preferences

3. Is data sent to a server?

In the current version, Monelyra is designed to store core app data on the user’s device by default. The app is not designed to automatically upload the user’s financial records, reminders, or personal tracking data to a developer-operated server.

If the user chooses to use backup, export, or sharing features, selected files or reports may leave the device through the method chosen by the user. Such sharing happens as a result of the user’s own action.

4. Notifications and reminders

The app may send local notifications for reminders created by the user. Notifications may be used for bills, rent, subscriptions, vehicle dates, loans, credit cards, or other personal tracking records. Notification behavior depends on device permissions, Android version, and system settings.

5. Backup, import, and export

Monelyra may allow users to back up, export, or later import their data. These files are under the user’s control. If the user shares, stores, or sends a backup file outside the device, this is done through the method selected by the user.

We recommend keeping backup files in a secure place and avoiding the import of backup files from unknown sources.

6. PDF and CSV reports

The app may generate PDF/CSV-style reports from financial or life-tracking data selected by the user. These reports are generated on the device and may be shared or stored by the user. Sharing reports with third parties is the user’s own choice.

7. Security and PIN protection

The app may provide local security layers, PIN protection, or device-based protection features on supported versions. These features may help reduce unauthorized access to app data, but device security, screen lock, and operating system settings remain the user’s responsibility.

8. Ads, analytics, and third-party SDKs

The current version is not intended to use third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral analytics SDKs. If advertising, analytics, or third-party services are added in the future, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declarations will be updated accordingly.

9. Data sharing

Monelyra is not designed to automatically send the user’s locally entered personal finance and life-tracking data to a developer server or automatically share it with third parties. If the user chooses to share a file, report, or backup, the sharing takes place through the app or service selected by the user.

10. Data retention and deletion

Because app data is generally stored on the user’s device, users may delete records inside the app or clear app data from device settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data may delete locally stored data.

11. Children’s privacy

Monelyra is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children. The app is designed for personal finance and life organization tracking.

12. User rights

Users may contact dasguardcore.notify@gmail.com for questions about privacy, data use, data deletion, or data stored in the app. Depending on your region, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection laws.

13. Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page. For significant changes, additional notice may be provided through the app or store listing.

14. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: dasguardcore.notify@gmail.com
